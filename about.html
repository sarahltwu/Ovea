About Ovea

We're two students building a safe, open community for girls, somewhere to share what you're going through and ask the questions you're too embarrassed to ask anywhere else.

Our story

We noticed how many girls didn't know how their periods worked, what was normal, or when something might be a sign that they needed help.

Yet there are so few places where girls can ask these questions openly. Stigma around periods, puberty, and our bodies can make even normal questions feel embarrassing.

So we created the space we wished we had.

Ovea gives girls a place to ask questions, share experiences, and learn about their bodies without judgment. Our Community is for girls to connect and share what they've been through, while Health Info provides clear, factual information from trusted medical sources.

No ads. No selling your data. Free, always :)

What we believe

01

No question is embarrassing

The things you're most afraid to ask are usually the things loads of other girls are wondering too.

02

Safety and anonymity first

People open up when they feel safe. Post anonymously whenever you want, and every post is moderated.

03

We show our sources

Shared experience is powerful, but it isn't medical advice. Our guides link every source, and a doctor confirms them before publishing.

04

Free, always

Nobody should have to pay to understand their own body. Donations keep Ovea open and free.
