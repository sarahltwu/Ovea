About Ovea

A safe, anonymous community where girls can ask the questions that feel too awkward to ask anywhere else.

Our story

There are questions about your own body that feel too embarrassing to say out loud, even to a friend. Search them instead and you get answers that are confusing, scary, or trying to sell you something. We built Ovea so girls have somewhere better to go.

The site has two sides. The community is girls talking to girls about what they're actually going through, anonymously if you want. We're students, not doctors, and nobody here will diagnose you. Health Info is the factual side: guides we write in plain language from sources like Mayo Clinic, the NHS, ACOG, and Planned Parenthood, each one linked so you can read further, and checked by a doctor before it goes up.

What we believe

01

02

03
