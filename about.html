About Ovea

We're a nonprofit building a safe, open community for girls, somewhere to share what you're going through and ask the questions you're too embarrassed to ask anywhere else.

Our story

There's still so much shame around girls' health. Periods, puberty, the way your body changes, all of it gets treated like something you're supposed to keep quiet about. So you end up embarrassed by things that are completely normal, holding questions you don't feel like you can say out loud to anyone.

So we made the place we wished we'd had. Ovea has two sides, and we keep them separate on purpose.

The community is girls talking to girls, anonymous if you want it to be, and moderated to stay kind. It's where you tell your story, hear from girls who've been through the same thing, and finally ask the thing you've been too embarrassed to ask. We're not doctors, and nobody here is going to diagnose you. What we can offer each other is honesty, experience, and knowing you're not the only one.

Health Info is the factual side. We write these guides ourselves, in plain language, from trusted medical sources we link directly so you can read further yourself, including Mayo Clinic, KidsHealth, the NHS, ACOG, and Planned Parenthood. A doctor checks each one before it goes up.

No ads. We don't sell your data. Free, for everyone.

What we believe

01

No question is embarrassing

The things you're most afraid to ask are usually the things loads of other girls are wondering too.

02

Safety and anonymity first

People open up when they feel safe. Post anonymously whenever you want, and every post is moderated.

03

We show our sources

Shared experience is powerful, but it isn't medical advice. Our guides link every source, and a doctor confirms them before publishing.

04

Free, always

Nobody should have to pay to understand their own body. Donations keep Ovea open and free.
