Hey, welcome to Ovea!

This is a space for girls and young women to talk about pretty much anything, ask the questions that feel too awkward to say out loud, and cheer each other on. You can post with a name or stay totally anonymous, whatever feels right for you.

1.Be kind and have each other's backs, every experience is welcome here
2.Share as much or as little as you want, just try not to post things that could identify you or someone else
3.No hate, bullying, or tearing people down
4.We're here to support each other, so share what helped you instead of giving medical advice
5.If things ever feel really heavy, our Get Help page has people ready to support you
